Robotic Ball Shooter

Autonomous target-finding robot with high-velocity projectile launcher

Built at a hackathon, this autonomous robot finds people using computer vision, drives toward them, and launches squash balls at high speed. Theoretically capable of firing at 100+ MPH — we didn't quite get there before the hackathon ended, but the targeting, locomotion, and launcher all worked. Built on the BracketBot ecosystem (self-balancing two-wheeled base) with a pneumatic/flywheel barrel on top.

Key Features

  • Autonomous Targeting: Computer vision pipeline detects and tracks people in real time, driving the robot toward its target.
  • High-Velocity Launcher: PVC barrel with high-speed flywheel mechanism — theoretically capable of 100+ MPH projectile velocity.
  • BracketBot Base: Self-balancing, two-wheeled mobile platform providing stable locomotion for dynamic environments.
  • Hackathon Build: Full system — vision, navigation, and launcher — designed and assembled in under 24 hours.
  • Projectile: Squash balls, chosen for their size, weight, and bounce characteristics.
